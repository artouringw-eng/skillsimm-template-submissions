# D1 — Security & Risk Management

- **Priority order:** human safety → follow policy/law → protect the business → technical fixes.
- **Quantitative risk:** SLE = AV × EF; ALE = SLE × ARO. Example: $200k × 25% = $50k SLE; once every 4 years → ARO 0.25 → ALE $12.5k.
- Senior management is ultimately accountable for risk.
